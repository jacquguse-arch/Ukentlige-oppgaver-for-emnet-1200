About Locked in Fitness

Who we are

Locked in Fitness is a modern gym built for everyone, no matter your experience level.

We believe strength training changes lives, both physically and mentally.

Our mission

We want people to see how good training is for the body and how much it can change people's lifes.

The owner and their partner struggled with self-worth when it came to their bodies. When they started focusing on lifting weights, they became more confident than ever — and they want everyone who walks through our doors to feel that too.

Our team

Our coaches are certified in strength training, boxing, and yoga, and are always ready to help you reach your goals.
